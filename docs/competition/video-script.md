All data shown is synthetic; say so in the captions. The demo numbers are
the real ones the build produces: 7 exceptions, GBP 15,110.00 at risk.

## Production notes

- Record at 2x zoom on the browser so text is legible on phones.
- The voiceover should be spoken, not read: rehearse twice against the timer.
- Captions mandatory; keep the £ figures on screen long enough to read.
- No client names, no real data anywhere.
- Cut the architecture slide if the video runs long; the demo carries it.
