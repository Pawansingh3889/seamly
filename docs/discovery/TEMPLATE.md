# UK mid-market discovery interview template

Goal: verify or break the PROBLEM.md hypothesis with real evidence from 10
to 15 UK mid-market companies (20 to 250M pounds turnover), before and
alongside the 18 October submission. Log one file per interview in this
directory using the template below; keep them anonymous unless the person
consents.

## Recruiting targets

- Financial controllers and CFOs at distributors, light manufacturers and
  food manufacturers
- Ops and logistics managers who own delivery records
- Systems context: Sage, Xero, NetSuite, D365 users, plus spreadsheet-heavy
  teams

## Interview script (30 minutes)

1. Context: company, turnover band, sector, finance team size. (2 min)
2. Systems: what runs finance, what runs operations/logistics, what lives
   in spreadsheets. How do records move between them? (5 min)
3. The seam: who reconciles orders, deliveries and invoices today? How
   often? How long does it take? What falls through? (8 min)
4. The last leak: when did money last get lost between systems? What was
   it worth? How was it found, and how long did it take? (8 min)
5. Pricing: what would visibility of this be worth per year? What would
   they pay? What must be true before they would trust the numbers? (5 min)
6. Close: may we come back with a demo? (2 min)
